Frequently Asked Questions (Hisense Pt1)

How far back does the Hisense Pt1 need to be for a 120-inch screen?

With a throw ratio of 1.2:1 to 1.5:1 and a 120-inch 16:9 screen width of 104.6 inches (8.72 feet), the Hisense Pt1 lens must be placed between 10.5 ft and 13.1 ft from the projection screen.

What distance is needed for a 100-inch screen on the Hisense Pt1?

A 100-inch 16:9 screen measures 87.2 inches (7.26 feet) wide. Multiplying by the throw ratio gives a required throw distance of 8.7 ft to 10.9 ft.

What is the ideal seating distance for a 120-inch screen with the Hisense Pt1?

Following SMPTE (30-degree field of view) and THX (40-degree cinema immersion) guidelines, the optimal viewing distance for a 120-inch 4K screen is between 11.8 feet and 16.0 feet.
